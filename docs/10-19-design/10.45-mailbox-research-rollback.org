The research model preserves the union of learned failures across rollbacks.
Zero rejection, clean shutdown and correctness do not waive tails or backlog.
An anomalous current control must stop candidate execution, not qualify an
improvement against a slow baseline. That launcher enforcement remains an open
implementation obligation: this publication does not claim it is wired in.

* Verification and next decision

=just mailbox-research-gate-check= passes the focused measurement (17), resource
(9), cost (3), research (28), layout (11) and aggregate (295) cases. Counts
overlap and are not a count of distinct native failures. Earlier isolated
mutation checks caught 28 weakened guards plus two layout protocol mutations.
No business benchmark is rerun for this publication. Model checks do not prove
the cause of historical stalls or certify native performance.

Further runtime research starts from 7F and the original Mailbox supply,
Actor execution and verified frame-return cycle. Each change must account for
the complete cycle, preserve immediate refill and per-result ownership, and
pass actual-VM correctness before a targeted original-workload comparison.
Confirmed regression removes that runtime change while preserving its evidence
and model witness. SDK, defaults, memory cap and acceptance limits stay fixed.
